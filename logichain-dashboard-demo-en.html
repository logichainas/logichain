How Logichain works

1

Last opp Excel

Suppliers send their reports in Excel. Logichain reads the files directly – no manual copying.

2

Logichain validerer og samler

Data is checked, standardised and consolidated into one model: orders, deliveries, deviations, prices and volumes.

Beslutninger
