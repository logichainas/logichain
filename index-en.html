The challenge

The answers already exist. Just not in one place.

Most purchasing teams sit on valuable data – scattered across Excel, email and supplier reports. That costs time, money and negotiating power.

No single view

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What do you need to get started?

Just Excel reports from your suppliers – in a simple template we set up together. No ERP integration and no IT project.

How often is the dashboard updated?

As often as new reports arrive – typically weekly or monthly. Every update is reviewed before it is published.

How do you use AI?

An AI analyst works through the data, finds deviations, price gaps and risk, and suggests actions. An analyst reviews and approves everything before it appears in your dashboard.

Can we trust the numbers?

Every estimate can be traced back to the materials, orders and assumptions behind it. We show identified potential worth investigating – never guaranteed savings.

Who owns and sees the data?

You own your data. Each customer can only access their own data, and you can export everything to Excel at any time.

Is Logichain a fit for smaller companies?

Yes. Small and mid-sized companies often keep purchasing data in Excel without any tooling – that's exactly where Logichain adds value fastest.

What does it cost?

Pricing is tailored to size and scope as a fixed subscription – a fraction of traditional BI platforms. You see the value in the demo before you decide.
