The challenge
Do you recognise this from your purchasing department?
Most companies are sitting on gold worth of purchasing data – without being able to use it. Here are the three most common symptoms.
📂

Scattered Excel files

Every supplier reports in its own format. Data is copied manually, arrives late and contains errors – and nobody has time to clean it up.

🌫️

No unified overview

How much do we actually buy from each supplier? Where are prices rising? Who is delivering late? The answers exist – but never in one place.

🎲

Decisions without facts

Follow-ups, negotiations and supplier selection are based on gut feeling – while documented numbers would make a completely different negotiation card.

How it works
Three steps. Days – not months.
Logichain requires no ERP integration and no IT project. Your suppliers keep working as before – in Excel – and Logichain does the rest.
1

Upload Excel

Suppliers send reports in a fixed, simple template – weekly or monthly. You upload the files, and you are up and running.

2

Logichain validates and consolidates

Data is checked and standardised automatically into one model: orders, deliveries, deviations, prices and volumes – across all suppliers.

3

Decisions

Executive overview, spend analysis, supplier ratings, identified savings potential and prioritised actions – ready for the management meeting.
